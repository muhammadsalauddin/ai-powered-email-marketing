Problem

Outreach is fragmented across multiple tools. Existing platforms assist but do not execute. Teams juggle lead sourcing, email writing, SMTP setup, sending, replies, and reporting without a unified workflow.

Solution

Crownvo's AI operator turns a prompt into a live campaign. The system handles strategy, content creation, automation building, campaign launch, reply management, and reporting through guided conversations.

Current Shipped Platform

  • Email Warmup, Lead Generation, Email Finder, Email Verifier

  • Reply and Inbox, Analytics, Owner Reports
  • Crownvo Mails Chrome extension, published free with 200+ founder-reported regular users
