Updated Pitch Deck Summary

Problem

Outreach is fragmented across multiple tools. Existing platforms assist but do not execute. Teams juggle lead sourcing, email writing, SMTP setup, sending, replies, and reporting without a unified workflow.

Solution

Crownvo's AI operator turns a prompt into a live campaign. The system handles strategy, content creation, automation building, campaign launch, reply management, and reporting through guided conversations.

Current Shipped Platform

  • Email Warmup, Lead Generation, Email Finder, Email Verifier
  • Email Editor, Campaign Engine, Multiple SMTP including personal Gmail
  • Reply and Inbox, Analytics, Owner Reports
  • Crownvo Mails Chrome extension, published free with 200+ founder-reported regular users

Next AI Operator Layer

  • AI Chat - conversational campaign creation
  • Telegram Command System - mobile-first approvals and status checks
  • AI Credit Control - credit-based usage and execution gate

Business Model and Pricing

Four active pricing tiers from GBP 15/mo to GBP 75/mo, with annual plans saving up to 33 percent. 14-day free trial for all tiers. Natural upgrade path from solo founders to high-volume operators.

Team

Shueb Kamil handles GTM and distribution. Muhammad Salauddin handles build, AI, and architecture.

Ask

Seeking GBP 20,000. Monthly burn of GBP 1,383. Funds will support distribution, AI operator development, infrastructure, and growth.
